# Terms of Service - Alma

**Last updated:** August 4, 2026

## 1. Overview
Alma is an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant designed to support students and educational staff within Gothenburg Municipality (*Göteborgs Stad*). These Terms of Service govern your access to and use of the Alma service. By accessing or using Alma, you agree to comply with these terms.

## 2. Intended Use
Alma is intended to assist users with educational and administrative tasks, general inquiries, content generation, and organizational workflows in Gothenburg municipal schools. Alma is available through two primary client interfaces:
- **Chrome Extension:** A web browser extension integrated into Google Chrome.
- **Google Chat (Workspace Add-on):** An internal add-on available within Google Workspace Chat.

## 3. Access Requirements
Access to Alma is strictly restricted to authorized users holding an active email account under the following Google Workspace domains:
- `@skola.goteborg.se`
- `@educ.goteborg.se`

Unauthorized access attempts or use by individuals outside these approved municipal domains are strictly prohibited.

## 4. User Responsibilities
Users must adhere to the following responsibilities when interacting with Alma:
- **Sensitive Data Protection:** Users must **not** input, submit, or transmit sensitive personal data, special category data (such as health information or social security numbers), confidential student records, or classified municipal data into conversation threads.
- **Appropriate Conduct:** Users must maintain professional and respectful language and comply with Gothenburg Municipality's IT governance policies and code of conduct.
- **Verification of Output:** Users are responsible for evaluating and verifying all information provided by Alma before using it in official contexts, coursework, or administrative decisions.

## 5. AI Service & Model Disclaimer
Alma utilizes Google Gemini AI technology to process queries and generate conversational responses. Please note:
- AI-generated responses are probabilistic and may occasionally contain inaccuracies, incomplete information, or unexpected outputs.
- Alma does not represent human expertise, official legal counsel, or binding municipal decisions.
- Utbildningsförvaltningen does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of any content generated by the AI assistant.

## 6. Service Availability
Alma is an internal municipal service provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis.
- There are no guaranteed uptime SLAs or commitments to uninterrupted service.
- Access may be temporarily modified, suspended, or discontinued for maintenance, updates, or technical reasons without prior notice.

## 7. Permissions and Access
To provide functionality across Chrome Extension and Google Chat interfaces, Alma requests identity verification via Google OAuth 2.0:
- Access permissions are granted strictly to verify domain eligibility (`@skola.goteborg.se` or `@educ.goteborg.se`) and route requests to the authorized backend.
- Administrative access rights may be revoked at any time by Utbildningsförvaltningen or system administrators.

## 8. Data Retention & Privacy
- **Conversation Logs:** Interaction logs and prompt history are retained for auditing, troubleshooting, and service improvement purposes for up to **90 days**, after which they are automatically purged or anonymized.
