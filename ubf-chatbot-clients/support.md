# Support - Alma (UBF Chatbot)

Welcome to the support page for **Alma**, the AI assistant developed by Utbildningsförvaltningen Göteborgs Stad for students and staff in Gothenburg municipal schools.

## About Alma
Alma is an internal AI assistant powered by Google Gemini, designed to assist users with learning, teaching, and administrative workflows. Alma is accessible via:
- **Chrome Extension** (for web browser integration)
- **Google Chat Add-on** (within Google Workspace)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Who is eligible to use Alma?
Alma is restricted to active students and staff within Gothenburg municipal schools holding Google Workspace accounts on `@skola.goteborg.se` or `@educ.goteborg.se` domains.

### 2. How do I access Alma?
- **Chrome Extension:** Install the official Alma Chrome extension distributed via the organization's managed Google Chrome Web Store.
- **Google Chat:** Add the Alma app directly inside Google Chat within your `@skola.goteborg.se` or `@educ.goteborg.se` Google Workspace account.

### 3. What kind of information can I share with Alma?
Alma is intended for general educational, administrative, and creative assistance. **Do not submit sensitive personal data**, confidential student records, social security numbers, or sensitive administrative information in your conversations.

### 4. Why did Alma provide an inaccurate or unexpected response?
Alma uses Google Gemini AI technology. While highly capable, AI models can sometimes generate incorrect or misleading answers. Always double-check facts and verify important information before using it for official purposes or schoolwork.

### 5. How long are conversation logs kept?
Conversation logs are retained for operational monitoring, troubleshooting, and safety auditing for up to **90 days**, after which they are automatically deleted or anonymized.
